I am a research lead at [Arcadia Alignment](https://www.arcadiaimpact.org/alignment). I'm mostly thinking about risks of automating alignment research, and how to measure differential speedup between alignment and capabilities R&D.

Previously I...

- was an Astra Fellow at [Redwood Research](https://redwoodresearch.org/), working on no-CoT time horizons.
- was a post-doc at the Alan Turing Institute on [Project Bluebird](https://www.turing.ac.uk/science-innovation/defence-and-national-security/project-bluebird), working on synthetic scenario generation for autonomous air-traffic control. I gave a [Pint of Science talk](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Rb1JxNABp2Q) about it.
- completed a PhD in mathematical physics at the University of Oxford. My [thesis](https://inspirehep.net/files/4e391dbbab57982966eb0252ec9b2cf9) was on *Generalized Symmetries in String Theory Realizations of Quantum Field Theories*. I wrote a [blog post](https://www.maths.ox.ac.uk/node/64281) about my research.
